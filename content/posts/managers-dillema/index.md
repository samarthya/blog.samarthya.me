> Do not handle adults with kids gloves

Some of the gems I hear everyday and the benefits are exceptional.

1. You need to be direct and do not gloss the message.

2. You know the organization is going to back you.

3. The other person also gets the opportunity to demonstrate the maturity he/she possesses.
