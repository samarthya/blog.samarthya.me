do?": what people actually read here, the code I keep on GitHub, and where to find me.

Tonight the story of how it was built went out, one part an hour, starting with
[part 1, Google Analytics]({{< relref "/posts/about-site-1-google-analytics" >}}). The part I'm proudest
of is [part 2]({{< relref "/posts/about-site-2-honest-numbers" >}}): my stats page wanted to tell me 58% of my
readers live in Singapore, and making it tell the truth took more work than the rest of the site.
