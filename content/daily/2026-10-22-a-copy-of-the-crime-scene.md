Took the first full copy of the old site's database today.

It was a strange thing to read. My own account was gone. **Every post, all 207 of them, belonged to the
attacker's account**, the one "registered" on 10 October 1970. 46 of my posts were sitting in drafts.

A database turns out to be a good witness: it remembers when plugins were switched off, when snippets were
created, and how many logins failed each hour. Most of this log comes from it.
