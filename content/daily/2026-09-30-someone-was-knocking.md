*Written on 26 October from the old site's own records. At the time, I didn't know any of this.*

The login-protection plugin counted **165 failed logins** today. Bots try passwords on every WordPress site,
all year round, so a few a day is normal. This was the busiest single day in its records.

Nothing got in today. But someone was knocking, and they were patient.
