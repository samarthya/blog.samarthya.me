*Written on 26 October from the old site's own records. At the time, I didn't know any of this.*

**21 successful logins** today, more than I'd make in a year. Early in the morning (UTC), the first spam post went up:
casino and betting "reviews" in German, Spanish, Greek, Turkish, Finnish… Over the next two days there were
**55** of them, published as if I'd written them.

That's what a hijacked blog is worth to someone: six years of reputation with search engines, rented out to
people selling gambling links.
