The old WordPress blog was compromised today. Someone got in, left files behind that had no business being
there, and the site stopped being something I could trust.

First moves: take it offline, change every password that touched it, and keep a copy of everything for later.
Six years of posts were in there. Tomorrow's problem is what can be saved.
